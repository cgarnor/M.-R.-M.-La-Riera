PoissonRecon.exe — Screened Poisson Surface Reconstruction, Version 18.76
https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~misha/Code/PoissonRecon/   https://github.com/mkazhdan/PoissonRecon

Kazhdan, M. & Hoppe, H. (2013). Screened Poisson Surface Reconstruction. ACM Transactions on Graphics, 32(3).

This is the same reconstruction library used by the CloudCompare PoissonRecon plugin. Its default
parameters (degree 1, Neumann boundary, scale 1.1, samplesPerNode 1.5, pointWeight 2, iters 8,
fullDepth 5) match CloudCompare's PoissonReconLib defaults.
